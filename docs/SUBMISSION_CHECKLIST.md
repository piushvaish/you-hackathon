## Judging criteria

- Completed loop: the demo result documents research, learning, external persistence, read-back, retry, and comparison.
- Technical implementation: CrewAI coordinates You.com, One/GitHub, and identical Daytona scoring for both runs.
- Innovation & Originality: persisted research-method learning controls the next answer to the same question.
- Impact & Relevance: evidence auditing addresses unsupported product comparisons.
- Presentation & Documentation: README, offline summary, sanitized result, and the video script explain the observed outcome.
